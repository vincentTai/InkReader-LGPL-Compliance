# InkReader Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

InkReader is a local-first ebook reader. The developer does not collect,
receive, sell, or share personal data through InkReader. The app contains no
analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.

## Books and reading data

Imported books and converted reading copies remain on the user's device and
are not uploaded by InkReader. The Share Extension temporarily copies a file
into InkReader's private App Group container so the main app can import it.

When the user is signed into iCloud, InkReader uses Apple's iCloud key-value
store to sync a book fingerprint, reading locator and progress, bookmarks, and
per-book reading preferences. Book files, book text, covers, titles, authors,
searches, and spoken text are not synced by InkReader. Apple processes iCloud
data under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Read Aloud uses voices and speech services provided by iOS. InkReader does not
send spoken book content to the developer.

## Network access

InkReader does not make third-party network requests. A user may explicitly
open the public open-source compliance link in their browser from Settings.
